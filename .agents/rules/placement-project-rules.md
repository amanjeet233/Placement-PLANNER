# Project Placement Guidelines & Rules

Whenever working in this workspace:

## 1. Zero Revision Videos for Aptitude
- Every quantitative aptitude resource must link to full conceptual courses.
- No revision marathon, one shot, or quick summary videos.

## 2. Dataset Separation
- DSA topics and questions reside in `dsa_roadmap_337_data.js`.
- Aptitude chapters and MCQs reside in `aptitude_roadmap_24_data.js`.
- Do not bloat `index.html` with massive raw datasets; keep them cleanly modularized.

## 3. UI/UX Quality (UI/UX Pro Max)
- Follow the 60-30-10 light theme design system.
- Ensure all cards have smooth hover interactions, legible typography (Outfit / Inter / JetBrains Mono), and WCAG AA contrast.
- Ensure 4-column responsive grid with dynamic size controls.

## 4. Single-Page App Integrity
- The portal has 11 views. Never remove or alter other views when editing a specific phase or topic.
